# MIZAN 

### Your Money. Your Clarity. Your Control.

MIZAN is an intelligent personal finance management platform designed to bring financial transactions from multiple payment sources into one unified ecosystem.

Our goal is to simplify personal financial management through automated transaction tracking, intelligent expense categorisation, financial analytics and collaborative expense splitting.

## The Problem

Modern users frequently make payments through multiple UPI applications, banking applications and other payment services.

This fragments transaction history and makes it difficult to understand spending patterns, track expenses and maintain financial awareness.

MIZAN aims to solve this problem by bringing financial information together in one place.

## Our Vision

To build a secure, intelligent and user-friendly financial companion that helps individuals understand, manage and improve their financial habits.

## Planned Features

- Unified Transaction Dashboard
- Automatic Transaction Detection
- Intelligent Expense Categorisation
- Monthly and Weekly Financial Analytics
- Expense Splitting and Group Management
- Budget Management
- Transaction Search and Filtering
- AI-powered Financial Insights
- Secure Local Storage and Cloud Synchronisation

## Development Philosophy

MIZAN is being developed as a real-world software product rather than a demonstration application.

Our development principles:

- Security and privacy by design.
- Modular and maintainable architecture.
- Reliable transaction processing.
- Meaningful testing and documentation.
- Collaborative development through GitHub.
- Continuous improvement through real-world usage.
